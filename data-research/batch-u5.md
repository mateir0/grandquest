# GrantQuest Data Pack — Batch U5: Asia + Middle East universities (2026-10-12)

## 1. KAIST Scholarship (South Korea)
Source: https://admission.kaist.ac.kr/intl-graduate/FinancialSupport/Scholarship/KAISTScholarship
Award: Full tuition for admitted international graduate students, plus national health insurance and a monthly stipend. Top applicants are considered for the KAIST Global Presidential Scholarship (full tuition + 1,000,000 KRW/month for 4 semesters) or the KAIST Prestige Scholarship for PhD students (full tuition + 300,000 KRW/month for 8 semesters).
Deadline: 2027 cycle — no exact dates published on the official page. The scholarship follows the KAIST admission cycles (Spring / Fall Early / Fall Regular). Status: not yet published.
Gates:
- Be an international applicant to a KAIST graduate program.
- Select the KAIST scholarship option as your financial resource in the admission application.
- Awardees are picked based on past performance and future promise in the application. No separate application needed.
Documents:
- No separate documents. The scholarship is considered together with your admission application.

## 2. SNU Global Scholarship (South Korea)
Source: https://en.snu.ac.kr/admission/graduate/scholarships/after_admission
Award: Covers tuition fees, living expenses, airfare, and housing costs for about 160 international graduate students. The exact amount depends on each student's situation.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — apply after you receive admission from SNU. No exact dates published on the official page. Status: not yet published.
Gates:
- Be an international student in an SNU graduate program.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source.

## 3. Yonsei GLD Freshman Scholarship — Outstanding Freshman Scholarship (South Korea)
Source: https://glc.yonsei.ac.kr/en/scholarship/
Award: Scholarship for outstanding international freshmen at Yonsei's Global Leaders College. The official page does not publish the exact amount. The student keeps it by meeting the academic conditions.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — no application process. You are considered automatically when you apply for admission. Status: not yet published.
Gates:
- Be an outstanding international student, picked based on your high school academic performance.
- Keep meeting the grade conditions to keep the scholarship.
Documents:
- None. There is no application process. You are considered with your admission application.

## 4. Schwarzman Scholars (China — master's at Tsinghua University)
Source: https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/admissions/
Award: Fully funded one-year master's at Tsinghua University in Beijing. Covers tuition and fees, room and board, travel to and from Beijing, in-country study tours, course books and supplies, a Lenovo laptop, health insurance, and a personal stipend. (Source for award: https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/frequently-asked-questions/)
Deadline: 2027 cycle — applications for the class of 2027–2028 are now closed. The next cycle (class of 2028–2029) opens April 2027 to September 2027. Status: upcoming.
Gates:
- Be 18 to 28 years old on August 1 of your enrollment year.
- Hold a bachelor's degree, completed before August 1 of your enrollment year.
- Show strong English skills. Test scores are waived if you studied in English for at least two years.
- Show leadership, character, and academic ability.
Documents:
- Online application form.
- CV, maximum 2 pages.
- Leadership essay, 750 words.
- Statement of purpose, 500 words.
- Official transcripts from every degree-granting school.
- Three letters of recommendation.
- One-minute video self-introduction.

## 5. Yenching Scholars (China — master's at Peking University)
Source: https://yenchingacademy.pku.edu.cn/ (homepage notice, September 2027 intake)
Award: The Yenching Fellowship. Covers tuition fees, on-campus accommodation, a monthly stipend, medical insurance, one round-trip travel stipend, and costs for designated research trips. (Fellowship details per the official PKU notice: http://oldisd.pku.edu.cn/info/1213/7099.htm, data labeled 2023 entry.)
Deadline: September 2027 intake — applications are open now. Deadline: November 30, 2026. Status: open.
Gates:
- Hold a bachelor's degree in any field, or be in your final year (degree awarded no later than August 31 of the enrollment year).
- Have an outstanding academic record.
- Show strong interest in the interdisciplinary study of China.
- Show leadership, extracurricular achievement, and community engagement.
- Meet the English requirement: IELTS 7.0, TOEFL iBT 100, Cambridge 185, CET-6 600, or TEM-4 pass. Waived for native English speakers or graduates of English-taught degree programs.
Documents:
- Online application form.
- Personal statement, 750 words or less.
- Statement of research interest, up to 1,500 words.
- CV.
- Official university transcripts.
- Diploma or certificate of enrollment.
- Two academic letters of recommendation, written by full or associate professors.
- English proficiency test report, if required.

## 6. NUS International Undergraduate Scholarship (Singapore)
Source: https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/scholarships/scholarships-for-freshmen-international-students/nus-international-undergraduate-scholarship
Award: 100% of subsidised undergraduate tuition fees (after the MOE Tuition Grant, which you must take up), S$5,800 annual living allowance, S$1,750 one-time computer allowance, S$5,000 annual accommodation allowance. Note: the MOE Tuition Grant binds international students to work for a Singapore-registered company for 3 years after graduation.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — no separate deadline. All eligible applicants are considered automatically through their NUS admission application. Interviews run January to July, with outcomes by mid-July. Status: upcoming.
Gates:
- Be a citizen of any country except Singapore.
- Have strong leadership qualities and potential.
- Have an outstanding co-curricular activities record.
- Have outstanding high school results.
- Apply for a full-time undergraduate degree program at NUS.
- Transfer applicants are not eligible.
Documents:
- None. You are considered through your undergraduate admission application.

## 7. NTU Nanyang Global Scholarship (Singapore)
Source: https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/scholarships/scholarship-opportunities/detail/nanyang-scholarship
Award: Full coverage of subsidised tuition fees (after Tuition Grant), S$6,500 living allowance per academic year, up to S$2,000 accommodation allowance per year, up to S$8,000 travel grant for an overseas program, S$2,000 one-off computer allowance, and enrolment in the NTU Honours College. No bond except the MOE Tuition Grant terms for international students.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — the scholarship application period is the same as the admission application period. Exact 2027 dates not published on the official page. Status: not yet published.
Gates:
- Open to all nationalities.
- Apply for a full-time undergraduate degree at NTU.
- Have outstanding academic results (A Level, IB, polytechnic diploma, NUS High School diploma, or Year 12 equivalent).
- Have excellent co-curricular records.
- Show strong leadership qualities and potential.
Documents:
- Online scholarship form, after your NTU admission application.
- Personal essay of not more than 250 words.
- Recent passport-size photo.
- A referee appraisal submitted online by your school teacher.

## 9. KAUST Fellowship (Saudi Arabia)
Source: https://admissions.kaust.edu.sa/phd-fellowship.html
Award: Every admitted MS and PhD student receives the KAUST Fellowship automatically. It covers full tuition, a monthly living allowance of US$20,000–30,000 per year, on-campus housing, medical and dental coverage, and relocation support.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — Fall 2027 entry (MS, MS/PhD, PhD): applications open September 28, 2026, close January 3, 2027. Spring 2027 entry (PhD only): closed September 27, 2026. (Source for deadlines: https://admissions.kaust.edu.sa/how-to-apply/admission-timelines) Status: open for Fall 2027.
Gates:
- Hold a relevant bachelor's degree (for MS) or master's degree (for PhD).
- Show a strong academic record.
- Meet the English requirement: TOEFL iBT 79 or IELTS 6.0, unless your degree is from an accredited university in the US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia, or New Zealand.
- Some students with external sponsorship may not be eligible for the fellowship.
Documents:
- Official university transcripts.
- CV.
- Statement of purpose.
- Contact details of 3 referees.
- Official English language test score, if available.
- Clear copy of your passport photo page.

## 10. MBZUAI Graduate Funding (UAE — master's and PhD)
Source: https://mbzuai.ac.ae/admissions/graduate-phd-admissions
Award: Funded students receive free tuition, a monthly stipend (AED 15,500/month for international MSc students, AED 17,500/month for international PhD students), one return airfare home each year, health insurance, and a UAE residence visa with Emirates ID. Note: PhD students are funded centrally in year 1, then by a faculty advisor. MSc students are fee-paying by default — merit-based funding is considered during the admissions review but is not guaranteed for every admitted student.
Deadline: Fall 2027 intake — portal opened September 7, 2026. Final deadline: December 15, 2026 (5 p.m. GST) for most programs. Decisions by March 31, 2027. Status: open.
Gates:
- Hold a STEM degree from a university recognized by the UAE Ministry of Education.
- Minimum CGPA of 3.0 or 3.5 out of 4.0, depending on the program.
- Show English proficiency (TOEFL iBT / IELTS / EmSAT). Waivers for citizens of the UK, USA, Australia, and New Zealand who studied there.
- Pass the online entry exam and a technical interview.
- For PhD: confirm a faculty advisor match by the end of year 1 to keep funding.
Documents:
- Online application form.
- CV.
- Statement of purpose, 500 to 1,000 words.
- Degree certificates and official transcripts in English (attested).
- English proficiency test certificate.
- GRE score (optional, counts as a plus).
- Referee recommendations (minimum 2 for MSc).
- Online entry exam and technical interview.
