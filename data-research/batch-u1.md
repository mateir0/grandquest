# GrantQuest Data Pack — Batch U1: UK universities (2026-10-12)

## 1. Clarendon Fund (Oxford)
Source: https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon
Award: Fully funded — full course fees plus a grant for living costs, for the full length of your course.
Deadline: 2027 intake — upcoming. There is no separate Clarendon deadline. You are considered automatically if you apply for your Oxford course by its funding deadline (December 2026 or January 2027, depending on the course).
Gates:
- You are applying for a new full-time or part-time Master's or DPhil course at Oxford.
- You are chosen for outstanding academic merit and potential.
- Open to all nationalities and all subjects.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source — there is no separate application. Your course application counts.

## 2. UCL Global Undergraduate Scholarship (UCL)
Source: https://www.ucl.ac.uk/scholarships/ucl-global-undergraduate-scholarship
Award: 10 awards cover full tuition fees plus a maintenance allowance (and extra money for visa and health surcharge costs). 23 awards cover full tuition fees only. The award lasts for the full length of your degree. Data labeled 2026 session.
Deadline: 2026 session — applications closed 5pm BST, Monday 27 April 2026 (closed). The 2027/28 cycle is not yet published.
Gates:
- You pay the overseas tuition fee rate.
- You come from a low-income background.
- You have applied for a full-time undergraduate degree at UCL for 2026/27.
Documents:
- If you are shortlisted, you must show financial evidence (usually around mid-May).
- Online scholarship application form via UCL's Portico system.

## 3. President's Scholarship (Imperial College London, undergraduate)
Source: https://www.imperial.ac.uk/giving/scholars/
Award: £1,000 per year of study, for up to four years.
Deadline: Not published by the official source — there is no application. Departments nominate students during the admissions process.
Gates:
- You are a new undergraduate student at Imperial.
- Your department picks you as one of the brightest and best students in your year.
Documents:
- None — there is no formal application process.

## 4. Edinburgh Global Undergraduate Mathematics Scholarship (University of Edinburgh)
Source: https://registryservices.ed.ac.uk/student-funding/undergraduate/international/mathematics
Award: £5,000 per year, paid in instalments as help with living costs, for the full length of your degree (you must make satisfactory academic progress).
Deadline: 2027/28 funding is not yet confirmed — not yet published. Data labeled 2026 session.
Gates:
- You have International or EU fee status.
- You are accepted for full-time admission to an undergraduate degree in the School of Mathematics.
- You are not already studying at Edinburgh.
- You show outstanding academic performance. This is a merit-based award.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source. You must have applied through UCAS, but you do not need an offer before starting the scholarship application.

## 5. Uggla Family Scholars Programme (LSE — replacement for King's)
Source: https://www.lse.ac.uk/study-at-lse/Undergraduate/fees-and-funding/uggla-family-scholarships/Uggla-Family-Scholarship-details
Award: Partial tuition fee award plus a full maintenance stipend — worth over £23,000 per year — for the full length of your degree. Includes guaranteed accommodation, coaching and careers support. Three awards are for overseas students each year.
Deadline: 2026 session — applications closed 30 April 2026 for overseas offer holders (closed). The 2027 cycle is not yet published.
Gates:
- You pay the overseas tuition fee rate.
- You hold a conditional or unconditional offer for an LSE undergraduate programme.
- You are from a less advantaged background. Selection is based on financial need.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source. You apply through the LSE Undergraduate Scholarships application form.

## 6. Global Futures Scholarship (University of Manchester)
Source: https://www.manchester.ac.uk/study/international/finance-and-scholarships/funding/global-futures-scholarship/
Award: Tuition fee discounts for 2027 entry. Undergraduate merit awards of up to £36,000 (£12,000 per year over three years). Over 350 awards in total, worth more than £6 million.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — open. Exact dates differ by country and region. You must hold an offer first; the application form is emailed to all eligible offer-holders.
Gates:
- You live in one of the listed countries (check your country page on the Manchester site).
- You hold an offer for a full-time, on-campus course in Manchester starting September 2027.
- You pay international fees and you fund your own studies (no sponsor, no full scholarship from elsewhere).
Documents:
- Not published by the official source. You apply with the online form that Manchester emails to offer-holders.

## 7. Chancellor's International Scholarship (University of Warwick)
Source: https://warwick.ac.uk/services/dc/schols_fund/new/cistc/
Award: Fully funded — full payment of academic fees (overseas rate), a maintenance stipend at the UK research council rate, and a £5,000 research training grant. Lasts up to 3.5 years of full-time doctoral study.
Deadline: The 2027 cycle is not yet published by the official source. Data labeled 2026 session: Warwick's own announcement for 2025/26 entry gave a deadline of 12 December.
Gates:
- You are applying for a postgraduate research course (PhD, MPhil/PhD or EngD) starting in October.
- You are assessed as overseas for fees.
- You are not already a research student at Warwick.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source. You apply for the course first, then submit a separate scholarship application.

## 8. Think Big Undergraduate Scholarship (University of Bristol)
Source: https://www.bristol.ac.uk/international/fees-finance/scholarships/
Award: £6,500 or £13,000 off your tuition fees, renewed each year for up to four years (you must make satisfactory progress). Data labeled 2026 session.
Deadline: 2026 session — applications closed 10 April 2026 at 10am UK time (closed). The 2027 cycle is not yet published.
Gates:
- You pay the overseas tuition fee rate.
- You have applied for an eligible full-time, in-person undergraduate course (Medicine, Dentistry and Veterinary courses are not eligible).
- You receive an offer and confirm Bristol as your firm UCAS choice.
- You meet or exceed your offer conditions.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source. You apply through one online international scholarships form.

## 9. Global Leadership Scholarship (University of Glasgow)
Source: https://www.gla.ac.uk/scholarships/globalleadershipscholarship/
Award: £10,000 off your tuition fees, for one year only. 350 scholarships available.
Deadline: 2027/28 session — upcoming. The scholarship will open shortly. It is assessed on a rolling basis; the closing date is not yet confirmed (usually late August).
Gates:
- You have International or EU fee status.
- You hold an offer for a full-time, 1-year Masters starting September 2027.
- You show academic excellence — grades equal to a UK 1st Class Honours degree.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source.

## 10. Postgraduate High Fliers Scholarship (University of Birmingham — replacement for Global Masters)
Source: https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/study/scholarships-funding/postgraduate-high-fliers-scholarship
Award: £5,000 off your Masters tuition fees.
Deadline: 2026/27 cycle — closing date 31 July 2026 (closed). The 2027/28 cycle is not yet published.
Gates:
- You have an offer for a full-time taught Masters at Birmingham's UK campus starting September 2026.
- You live in one of the listed countries and you pay overseas fees.
- You are not sponsored by an outside organisation (a third party paying your tuition fees).
Documents:
- None — there is no separate application. If you are eligible, the discount is applied automatically. Accept your offer and pay your deposit by the deadline in your offer letter.
