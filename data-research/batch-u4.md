# GrantQuest Data Pack — Batch U4: Europe universities (2026-10-12)

All facts below were checked against the university's own official pages. Deadlines are for the 2027 intake unless labeled otherwise.

## 1. Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship (TU Delft, Netherlands)
Source: https://www.tudelft.nl/en/delft-university-fund/we-support/support-innovative-education
Award: Two-year scholarship of approx. €60,000 for non-EU students and approx. €28,000 for EU students — full tuition fees plus a contribution to living expenses. 16 awards per year (2 per faculty).
Deadline: 1 December 2026 (23:59 CET) — 2027 intake, upcoming. (Official TU Delft pages confirm the annual December 1 deadline; the official 2027 detail page could not be loaded.)
Gates:
- You are an international applicant admitted to a 2-year regular TU Delft MSc programme (not a joint programme).
- You have excellent grades, roughly among the top 10% of your previous studies.
- You are new to TU Delft and did not get your bachelor's at a Dutch university.
Documents:
- Complete TU Delft MSc application.
- TU Delft Scholarship Application Form.
- Reference letter(s).
- English language certificate (non-EU/EFTA applicants).

## 2. Amsterdam Merit Scholarship (University of Amsterdam, Netherlands)
Source: https://www.uva.nl/en/about-the-uva/organisation/faculties/faculty-of-social-and-behavioural-sciences/education/social-sciences/scholarships.html
Award: Equal to the tuition fee, paid in monthly instalments (you still pay tuition yourself first). At Economics and Business the award is tuition fee plus a €5,000 allowance. Amounts and deadlines differ per faculty.
Deadline: 15 February 2027 (23:59 CET) — 2027 intake, open (Graduate School of Social Sciences; Economics and Business closes 15 January).
Gates:
- You hold a non-EU/EEA passport.
- You are admitted to a UvA Master's programme.
- You have excellent academic results.
- You are not eligible for Dutch student finance, and you hold no other full scholarship for the same period.
Documents (one single PDF, Social Sciences):
- Curriculum vitae (CV).
- Scholarship motivation letter.
- Letter of recommendation from a professor.
- Financial plan.

## 3. Radboud Scholarship Programme (Radboud University, Netherlands)
Source: https://www.ru.nl/en/education/scholarships/radboud-scholarship-programme
Award: Tuition reduced to €2,771 per year (instead of about €19,000). Also covers visa, residence permit, health and liability insurance. Living costs are NOT covered. Maximum 26 awards for 2027/2028.
Deadline: Submit in OSIRIS by 31 January 2027 (23:59 CET); application must be complete by 14 February 2027 — 2027 intake, open.
Gates:
- You hold a non-EU/EEA passport.
- Your bachelor's degree is from outside the Netherlands, and you never studied in the Netherlands.
- You meet the English requirement of your chosen Master's programme.
- You are fully admitted to an English-taught Master's starting 1 September 2027.
Documents:
- Two reference letters.
- Curriculum vitae (CV).
- Uploaded in the OSIRIS admission system when you tick the scholarship box.

## 4. Leiden University Excellence Scholarship (LExS) (Leiden University, Netherlands)
Source: https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/scholarships/sea/leiden-university-excellence-scholarship-lexs
Award: Partial tuition reduction — €10,000, €15,000, €17,500, €18,500 or €19,000 off the tuition fee, for the full programme. This is not a full scholarship — you still pay part yourself. About 25 awards per year.
Deadline: 1 December 2026 — 2027 intake, upcoming.
Gates:
- You hold a non-EEA/non-EFTA passport (exceptions: all nationalities for Advanced LLMs and MSc International Relations and Diplomacy).
- You apply for a full-time Master's starting in September (not a Law School non-advanced LLM or MSc).
- You have excellent grades, roughly top 10% of your previous studies.
Documents:
- Leiden admission application (you apply for the scholarship inside it).
- LExS motivation letter, PDF, max 500 words.

## 5. Maastricht University NL-High Potential Scholarship (Maastricht University, Netherlands)
Source: https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/studeren/toelating-inschrijving/financing-your-studies/scholarships/maastricht-university-nl-high
Award: Fully funded: full tuition, living expenses (€20,150 for a 13-month programme or €38,750 for a 25-month programme), health and liability insurance, visa costs, and an allocated room. Flights are NOT included. Only about 2% of applicants get it.
Deadline: Scholarship application open 1 October 2026 – 1 February 2027 (23:59 CET) — 2027 intake, open. You must submit your full Master's admission file by 10 December 2026.
Gates:
- You are not an EU/EEA, Swiss or Surinam national, and you have no dual EU/EEA/Swiss nationality.
- You are admitted to a participating full-time UM master's programme for 2027-2028.
- You never did degree studies in the Netherlands before.
- You are not older than 35 on 1 September 2027.
- Your GPA is at least 7.5 out of 10 (or 75% / 3 out of 4).
Documents:
- CV (max 2 A4 pages).
- Motivation letter (use the official UM template).
- Transcript and proof of GPA, plus a document explaining your grading system.
- Personal statement of financial need.
- One reference letter (dated after February 2026) and your referee's contact details.

## 6. Lund University Global Scholarship (Lund University, Sweden)
Source: https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/study/admission-degree-studies/scholarships-and-awards
Award: Tuition fee waiver of 25%, 50%, 75% or 100%. Living costs are NOT covered.
Deadline: 2027 intake — not yet published by the official source (status: upcoming). You are invited to apply for the scholarship after the admissions round closes.
Gates:
- You are a citizen of a country outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland.
- You must pay tuition fees (you are a fee-paying student).
- You made a complete application for a Master's programme at Lund (a few Bachelor's programmes also qualify).
Documents:
- Not published by the official source.

## 7. Uppsala University Global Scholarship (Uppsala University, Sweden)
Source: https://www.uu.se/en/study/masters-studies/scholarships/uppsala-university-scholarships
Award: Full tuition fee for an international Master's programme. Living expenses are NOT covered. Only about 2% of applicants get a scholarship.
Deadline: 2027 intake — the next round opens in early 2027; exact dates not yet published by the official source (status: upcoming).
Gates:
- You are a citizen of a country outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland.
- Your chosen Uppsala Master's programme is ranked first on universityadmissions.se.
- You applied before the University Admissions deadline and meet the entry requirements.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source (you apply via an online scholarship form after your programme application).

## 8. KTH Scholarship (KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Sweden)
Source: https://www.kth.se/en/studies/master/admissions/scholarships/kth-scholarship-1.72827
Award: Full tuition fee for a one- or two-year master's programme (year 2 needs satisfactory results). Living costs are NOT covered.
Deadline: Opens 1 December 2026, closes 15 January 2027 — 2027 intake, upcoming (opens soon). Results announced 1 April 2027.
Gates:
- You are a fee-paying student.
- You applied for a KTH master's programme as your FIRST priority.
- You paid the application fee.
- You meet the admission requirements of your programme.
Documents:
- No extra documents. KTH uses the documents from your programme application (supporting documents deadline: 2 February).

## 9. Emile Boutmy Scholarship (Sciences Po, France)
Source: http://www.sciencespo.fr/students/en/fees-funding/bursaries-financial-aid/emile-boutmy-scholarship/
Award: Master's: €18,500 tuition exemption per year, for each of the two years of the Master's programme. (Undergraduate: full or €9,500 exemption.)
Deadline: 2027 intake — Master's: 18 October (1st committee) or 29 November (2nd committee) — status: open. (The official page does not print the year; undergraduate deadlines on the same page are dated 2027.)
Gates:
- You are a first-time applicant to Sciences Po.
- You are from a non-EU state, and your household does not file taxes in the EU.
- You are admitted to an Undergraduate or Master's programme. (Excluded: Swiss/Norwegian citizens, EU dual citizens, Quebec master's candidates, journalism, 1-year master's, most dual degrees, exchange students, PhD.)
Documents:
- Mark the Emile Boutmy option in the "Financial information" section of your Sciences Po application.
- Language test (if required), attached before the deadline.
- Complete files only — late or incomplete files are not accepted.

## 10. International Master's Scholarship (Université Paris-Saclay, France)
Source: https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en/admission/bourses-et-aides-financieres/international-masters-scholarships-program-idex
Award: €10,000 per year, paid monthly from September to June, plus up to €900 for travel and visa costs. Renewed for a second year for M1 laureates with good results. Paid only after you arrive in France.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — not yet published by the official source (status: upcoming). Latest published cycle data labeled 2026 session: coordinator selection 25 March 2026, scholarship application deadline 31 March 2026, results mid-May 2026.
Gates:
- You are a foreign national admitted to a Paris-Saclay Master's (M1 or M2, not a vocational programme).
- This is your first enrolment at a French higher-education institution.
- You are under 30 years old during the application year.
- You do not receive other funding above €600 per month.
Documents:
- You are invited by email to the scholarship form after course coordinators select you — you cannot apply directly.
- Two referees may be asked to submit recommendations online.

## Dropped / replaced

- **#3 Utrecht Excellence Scholarship → replaced by Radboud Scholarship Programme.** Reason: officially discontinued. The official uu.nl page states the Utrecht Excellence Scholarship "will no longer be offered for programmes starting in the 2026–2027 academic year" due to budget cuts — "No new UES applications will be accepted." Radboud Scholarship Programme is the closest same-demand replacement (Dutch flagship, international Master's students, verified on official ru.nl page).
- **#2 Amsterdam Excellence Scholarship → replaced by Amsterdam Merit Scholarship.** Reason: the Amsterdam Excellence Scholarship no longer exists on official UvA sources (the old AES page returns an error). The current UvA flagship award for non-EU students is the Amsterdam Merit Scholarship (AMS), verified on official uva.nl pages. Note: amounts and deadlines differ per UvA faculty.
