# GrantQuest Data Pack — Batch U2: USA universities (2026-10-12)

## 1. Karsh International Scholarship, Duke University (USA)
Source: https://ousf.duke.edu/merit-scholarships/karsh-international-scholars-program/
Award: Full undergraduate funding for four years. Covers tuition, room, board, and mandatory fees. Also covers any demonstrated need beyond those costs. Gives funding for three summers of research, unpaid internships, and other academic opportunities.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — apply through Duke's admission process: Early Decision by November 2, 2026, or Regular Decision by January 4, 2027. Financial aid forms (CSS Profile) are due November 15, 2026 for Early Decision or February 1, 2027 for Regular Decision (data labeled 2026-2027 admission cycle, Duke official). Status: open.
Gates:
- You are an international applicant. You must show financial need.
- There is no separate application. Apply to Duke University and request financial aid.
- You must not already hold a bachelor's degree.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source. Documents are the standard Duke admission application and financial aid forms.

## 2. Ingram Scholars Program, Vanderbilt University (USA)
Source: https://www.vanderbilt.edu/scholarships/ingram/
Award: Full tuition, all required fees, and the value of on-campus housing each year. Plus a stipend for a special summer service project. The scholarship can be renewed each year through senior year if you keep a 3.0 GPA and meet the service requirements. Extra need-based aid is available if your need is higher than full tuition.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — December 1, 2026, for prospective first-year students. Status: open.
Gates:
- You are chosen for your commitment to community service, your entrepreneurial spirit, your personal character, and your leadership potential.
- First, apply to Vanderbilt University. Then apply electronically to the Ingram Scholars Program through your MyAppVU account.
- Finalists must do an interview with the selection committee.
- No citizenship restriction is stated on the official page.
Documents:
- The Ingram Scholars Program application, submitted through MyAppVU along with your admission application.
- A full document list is not published by the official source. Finalists complete an interview.

## 3. Robert W. Woodruff Scholarship, Emory University (USA)
Source: https://apply.emory.edu/financial-aid/scholar-program
Award: Emory's top merit scholarship. Covers full tuition, required fees, and on-campus room and meals. Can be renewed for up to eight semesters of undergraduate study. Scholars also get special programming, advising, and summer funding opportunities.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — November 1 (Early Decision I Scholar Program deadline) or November 15 (Early Decision II and Regular Decision Scholar Program deadline). Status: open.
Gates:
- Open to first-year, homeschooled, and international applicants, regardless of citizenship.
- Choose Early Decision I, Early Decision II, or Regular Decision.
- Answer "Yes" to the Scholars question on the Emory application. You will not be considered otherwise.
- About 10,000 students apply each year; 175 to 200 become finalists.
Documents:
- No separate documents. Your Emory admission application is your scholarship application.

## 4. Jefferson Scholarship, University of Virginia (USA)
Source: https://www.jeffersonscholars.org/scholarships
Award: Covers the entire cost of attendance for four years at the University of Virginia, plus extra enrichment experiences. Students from outside Virginia get over $83,000 per year, which is over $405,000 in total.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — December 1, 2026, for both counselor and nominee materials. Status: open.
Gates:
- You cannot apply directly. Your school must nominate you. Eligible schools each nominate one senior.
- Nominees show excellence in leadership, scholarship, and citizenship.
- Your citizenship status is not considered. International students can be nominated.
- Financial need is not a factor in selection.
Documents:
- A scholastic report, extracurricular activities list, and two essays.
- School officials submit a transcript, school profile, and one letter of recommendation.
- You must also submit a separate undergraduate admission application to UVA.

## 5. Morehead-Cain Scholarship, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (USA)
Source: https://www.moreheadcain.org/
Award: A four-year undergraduate merit scholarship. Covers tuition, books, and room and board. Also funds four summers of travel and experiential learning, plus Discovery Fund grants for extra educational experiences.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — October 1, 2026, for the Morehead-Cain application. Admission to UNC is separate: October 15, 2026 for Early Action, or January 1, 2027 for Regular Decision (data labeled 2026-2027 cycle, official). Status: closed for the 2027 cycle — the October 1 deadline has passed.
Gates:
- You must be on track to graduate from high school in the spring.
- Be nominated by your high school or an affiliate program, or apply independently.
- Be a competitive applicant for admission to UNC-Chapel Hill. Admission is a separate process.
- Selection is based only on merit: leadership, moral force of character, academic achievement, and physical vigor.
Documents:
- The Morehead-Cain application.
- Your counselor submits a school report form and a recommendation form. All counselor materials are due by the same October 1 deadline.

## 6. Eugene McDermott Scholars Program, University of Texas at Dallas (USA)
Source: https://provost.utdallas.edu/mcdermott-programs/eugene-mcdermott-scholars/
Award: A four-year, full-ride undergraduate merit scholarship. Covers tuition, housing, living costs, and educational stipends. Also funds study abroad. No financial need is required to get this award.
Deadline: December 1 — UT Dallas application with supplemental documentation due, and McDermott Scholars Program application with two recommendation forms due (data labeled 2026 session; the 2027 cycle dates are not yet published on the official source). Status: upcoming.
Gates:
- You are a first-time first-year student. You start college in the fall right after high school. No gap years. No transfer students.
- Open to both domestic and international students.
- Apply to UT Dallas first to get your NetID, then apply to the McDermott program by the deadline.
- Be in the top academic tenth of the UT Dallas entering class of 2027 and get offered an Academic Excellence Scholarship award.
- About 16 to 20 scholars are chosen each year.
Documents:
- The complete McDermott application.
- Two McDermott Recommendation Forms, one from each of your two recommenders.
- Your UT Dallas admission application with supplemental documentation.

## 7. Boston University Presidential Scholarship (USA)
Source: https://www.bu.edu/admissions/tuition-aid/scholarships-financial-aid/first-year-merit/
Award: Covers half of your tuition each year. Given to first-year students from around the world with exceptional academic achievement. Award is effective for students entering from Fall 2026 onward.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — December 1. To be considered, you must complete your application for admission by December 1. Status: open.
Gates:
- Open to US citizens, permanent residents, and international students.
- For first-year undergraduate applicants only.
- No separate scholarship application. Just complete your admission application by December 1.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source beyond the admission application itself.

## 8. Harvard University Need-Based Financial Aid for International Students (USA)
Source: http://college.harvard.edu/financial-aid/how-aid-works
Award: Need-based aid only. Harvard meets 100% of your demonstrated financial need. International students get exactly the same aid as American students. Aid is given as grants and student jobs, not required loans.
Deadline: 2027-2028 school year — Restrictive Early Action: November 1 (financial aid materials). Regular Decision: February 1 (financial aid materials). Status: open.
Gates:
- Get admitted to Harvard College. Harvard admission is need-blind. Your financial need never affects your chance of admission.
- Apply for financial aid. All students follow the same process, regardless of citizenship.
- Aid is based only on need, not on grades or merit.
Documents:
- The CSS Profile.
- An IDOC packet with your family's financial documents. US citizens and permanent residents also submit the FAFSA.

## 9. Yale University Need-Based Financial Aid for International Students (USA)
Source: https://finaid.yale.edu/apply/prospective
Award: Need-based aid only. Yale meets 100% of your demonstrated financial need with grants that you never pay back. No loans are required. International students get the same need-based aid as domestic students. Awards can include housing, meals, and travel to and from campus.
Deadline: 2027-2028 priority deadlines (official) — Early Action (international citizens): December 1, 2026. Regular Decision: February 15, 2027. Status: open.
Gates:
- Get admitted to Yale College. Yale is need-blind for all students, including international students. Applying for aid never hurts your chance of admission.
- On your admissions application, say you want to be considered for need-based financial aid. Yale's financial aid office will then contact you with next steps.
- International students who enroll do not need to reapply for aid every year.
Documents:
- The CSS Profile.
- Supporting financial documents, which Yale requests directly from you. Note: Yale does not accept the ISFAA form. A full document list is not published on the official page.

## 10. Princeton University Need-Based Financial Aid for International Students (USA)
Source: http://admission.princeton.edu/apply/international-students
Award: Need-based aid only. Princeton meets the full need of every admitted international student, just like it does for US students. Aid comes as grants, not loans. Princeton does not offer merit or athletic scholarships.
Deadline: 2027 cycle — apply for admission by November 1 (Single-Choice Early Action) or January 1 (Regular Decision). Apply for financial aid along with your application. A separate financial aid deadline is not published on the official source. Status: open.
Gates:
- Get admitted to Princeton. Princeton admission is need-blind. There is no disadvantage for students who apply for aid, including international students.
- All applications are reviewed the same way, regardless of citizenship or country of residence.
- Apply for financial aid through Princeton's online financial aid application.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source. Princeton's online financial aid application collects your family's financial information.
