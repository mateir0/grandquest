# GrantQuest Data Pack — Batch U3: Canada + Australia universities (2026-10-12)

## 1. McGill Major Entrance Scholarships for International Students (Canada)
Source: https://www.mcgill.ca/studentaid/node/111/node/97
Also: https://www.mcgill.ca/studentaid/scholarships-aid/future-undergrads/entrance-scholarships/application-instructions
Award: Merit scholarship of $3,000, $5,000 or $10,000 per year for international students, renewable for up to 3–4 years (up to $40,000 total). The $12,000-per-year level is only for Canadian citizens and permanent residents.
Deadline: 2027 intake — applications open Oct 1, 2026; scholarship application deadline Jan 21, 2027 for applicants from schools outside Canada (admission deadline Jan 15, 2027). Status: upcoming.
Gates:
- First-time university student entering a full-time undergraduate degree at McGill.
- Top academic grades.
- Strong leadership in school or community activities (more than just taking part).
Documents:
- List of your school activities and community/volunteer work for the last three years.
- One general essay (about a person who inspired you, or an event that changed your outlook).
- One-page letter of reference that you write about yourself, in the third person.
- Two letters of reference (one academic, one about your activities), sent directly by the referees.
- A signed statement from your school (if your school provides one).

## 2. UBC International Scholars Program (Canada)
Source: https://undergraduatescholarships.ubc.ca/prog/2027_international_scholars_program_application/
Award: Need-and-merit-based award for international undergraduates. UBC does not publish fixed amounts on its official pages — the value is set from your family's demonstrated financial need. The program groups four awards: the Karen McKellin International Leader of Tomorrow Award, the Donald A. Wehrung International Student Award, the International Impact Award, and the Vantage One Excellence Award.
Deadline: 2027 intake — applications opened Oct 5, 2026; close Nov 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. Status: open.
Gates:
- International student entering your first undergraduate degree at UBC.
- Strong academic results.
- Shown leadership qualities and real contributions to your community.
- Need real financial help to be able to study at UBC.
- Nominated by your school or institution.
Documents:
- Completed award application form.
- Nomination form completed by your school or institution.
- Teacher referee form (you invite your nominator and referee through the portal).
- The required information collected in your application (the official page does not publish a full document list).

## 3. University of Waterloo International Student Entrance Scholarship (Canada)
Source: https://uwaterloo.ca/health/recognition-reception-awards
Award: Twenty scholarships of $10,000 each for outstanding international students admitted to Year One of an undergraduate degree. Data labeled: latest published on an official uwaterloo.ca listing; the current main scholarships page no longer lists this award — 2027 intake details are not yet published.
Deadline: Not published by the official source. Status: not yet published for 2027 intake.
Gates:
- International student admitted to Year One of an undergraduate degree program.
- Admission average of 90% or above.
- No separate application is required — you are considered automatically.
Documents:
- None — no application is required.

## 4. University of Alberta International Admission Scholarship (Canada)
Source: https://www.ualberta.ca/en/admissions/tuition-and-scholarships/international-entrance-scholarships/index.html
Award: Up to CAN $10,000 based on your admission average. It is admission-based — you are assessed automatically when you apply; no separate scholarship application is needed. Note: UAlberta states it does not offer full scholarships for international students. Bigger application-based options (e.g. President's International Distinction Scholarship, up to CAN $120,000 over 4 years) have a separate application.
Deadline: 2027 intake — no separate scholarship deadline; you are assessed automatically with your admission application. Status: open (admission-based). For application-based international scholarships, the deadline is Jan 10, 2027 (application opened Oct 1, 2026) — status: open.
Gates:
- Entering the first year of an undergraduate degree directly from high school.
- Strong admission average (top students receive this award).
Documents:
- Not published by the official source — you are considered automatically on admission.

## 5. University of Calgary International Entrance Scholarship (Canada)
Source: https://ucalgary.ca/registrar/awards/university-calgary-international-entrance-scholarship
Award: $20,000 per year, renewable in the second, third and fourth year. To renew, you need a GPA of 2.60 or higher over at least 24 units in the previous fall and winter terms, and you must still pay international tuition fees. Two awards are given each year.
Deadline: Not published by the official source. Status: not yet published for 2027 intake.
Gates:
- International student who pays international tuition fees (not a Canadian citizen or permanent resident).
- Entering the first year of any undergraduate degree in the fall term.
- Met the university's English Language Proficiency requirement.
- Strong academic merit.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source.

## 6. Melbourne International Undergraduate Scholarship (Australia)
Source: https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/melbourne-international-undergraduate-scholarship
Award: From 2027, a 20% tuition fee sponsorship for the full length of your course. The number of scholarships rises to 225 from 2027.
Deadline: No separate deadline — you are automatically considered when you apply for your course. Status: open for automatic consideration.
Gates:
- Citizen of a country with a GDP per capita of US$10,000 or less (World Bank data) — check the eligible-countries list on the official page.
- Received an offer for an Overseas Fee (international) place in a bachelor's degree starting in the year the scholarship is offered.
- Have not done any university-level study before (year-12 extension studies don't count).
- Scholarships go to the highest-ranked students based on final secondary school or foundation results.
Documents:
- None — automatic consideration with your course application.

## 7. ANU Chancellor's International Scholarship (Australia)
Source: https://study.anu.edu.au/scholarships/find-scholarship/anu-chancellors-international-scholarship
Award: A 25% or 50% tuition fee waiver for the full length of your degree. ANU checks you across 9 categories based on your citizenship and always considers you for the highest-value award you qualify for.
Deadline: Not published by the official source — there is no separate application; ANU assesses you automatically when you apply for admission. Status: not applicable (no separate scholarship deadline published).
Gates:
- International (overseas) student starting an undergraduate or postgraduate coursework degree at ANU.
- Not entering the Doctor of Medicine and Surgery or the Master of Military and Defence Studies (these are excluded).
Documents:
- Not published by the official source — no separate scholarship application exists.

## 8. University of Sydney Vice-Chancellor's International Scholarship (Australia)
Source: https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/e/vice-chancellor-international-scholarships-scheme.html
Award: One-off tuition payments in the first year, in five tiers based on your ranking: $60,000 or $40,000 paid over one year (two instalments), or $20,000, $10,000 or $5,000 paid for one semester. Not renewable after the first year.
Deadline: The official terms do not publish exact offer-round dates — you must accept your unconditional offer by the deadline of the relevant scholarship offer round. Status: not yet published.
Gates:
- International student.
- Have applied for, but not yet started, a bachelor's degree or coursework master's degree at the University of Sydney.
- Not receiving an Australian Government scholarship.
- Awarded on academic merit from your admission application.
Documents:
- Not published by the official source — no separate application; a selection committee assesses your admission application.

## 9. UNSW International Scientia Coursework Scholarship (Australia)
Source: https://www.scholarships.unsw.edu.au/scholarships/id/1988
Award: Two offers. Offer 1: full tuition fees for the minimum length of your program. Offer 2: $20,000 per year paid directly toward tuition fees for the minimum length of your program.
Deadline: 2027 Term 1 intake — application opened 06 July 2026, closes 30 October 2026 at 11:59 PM AEST. You must hold an offer of admission by 30 October 2026. Status: open.
Gates:
- International student starting full-time study in a UNSW undergraduate or postgraduate coursework degree in Term 1, 2027 (UNSW Online and UNSW Canberra are excluded).
- Received an offer of admission by 30 October 2026.
- Selected on academic merit, leadership, extracurriculars, and your reasons for studying at UNSW.
Documents:
- Online scholarship application with evidence for each selection point: academic merit; leadership skills (school, workplace or community); extracurricular activities (sports, culture, volunteering or work); your reasons for studying at UNSW. Claims without supporting documents are not assessed.

## 10. Monash International Merit Scholarship (Australia)
Source: https://www.monash.edu/study/fees-scholarships/scholarships/find-a-scholarship/international-merit-5770
Award: $15,000 per year toward tuition for the length of your degree — up to $75,000 total. Twenty scholarships are given each year. Recipients are also invited to apply for Monash Minds, a first-year leadership program.
Deadline: No separate deadline — students with a Monash undergraduate course offer are automatically considered. Status: open for automatic consideration.
Gates:
- International student.
- Received a full Monash course offer with no conditions.
- Plan to enrol full-time in an undergraduate degree at a Monash campus in Australia.
- Selected on academic achievement.
- Not eligible if you are: a current Monash student; studying Australian Year 12; starting the Bachelor of Medical Science and Doctor of Medicine; in a Monash pathway program; or transferring from another Monash campus or another Australian university.
- To keep the scholarship, maintain a 70% (distinction) average each semester.
Documents:
- None — automatic consideration.
