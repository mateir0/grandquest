## 1. Chevening Scholarship (UK)
Source: https://www.chevening.org/resource-hub/guidance/eligibility/
Gates:
- Citizen of a Chevening-eligible country or territory.
- Commit to returning to your home country for at least two years after the scholarship ends.
- At least two years' work experience after your undergraduate degree (equivalent to 2,800 hours).
- Undergraduate degree qualifying you for a UK master's; finished undergrad at least two years before the application deadline, certificate in hand by interview.
- Apply to three different eligible UK university courses; hold an unconditional offer from at least one by the timeline deadline.
- Not a British/dual British citizen (Bermuda/BN(O) exceptions apply); not resident in the UK at application.
Documents:
- Four written essays: leadership & influence, networking, study in the UK, career plan.
- Two reference letters (names at application; letters uploaded if shortlisted).
- Degree certificate and academic transcripts.

## 2. Eiffel Excellence Scholarship (France) — data labeled 2026 session
Source: https://www.campusfrance.org/en/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-program
Gates:
- Foreign nationality only; dual nationals holding French nationality are not eligible.
- Master's: no more than 29 at selection (born after March 1996 for the 2026 session).
- PhD: no more than 35 at selection (born after March 1990 for the 2026 session).
- Applications accepted ONLY from French higher education institutions — students cannot apply directly.
- Enrolling in a master's or PhD programme at a French higher education institution.
Documents (official file structure — the nominating institution assembles the file):
- Part 1: Candidate information (mandatory fields + attachments).
- Part 2: Presentation of the candidacy (mandatory fields + attachments).
- Part 3: Institution information (mandatory fields + attachments).
Note: student-level items (CV, transcripts, motivation) sit inside the institution's online form and are not publicly enumerated — do not invent them.

## 3. Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals (SISGP)
Source: https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals/
Gates:
- Citizen of one of the 34 eligible countries (residence there not required at application).
- Applied for a master's programme eligible for the SI scholarship.
- Liable to pay tuition fees to Swedish universities.
- At least 3,000 hours of demonstrated work experience (max three employers); Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Moldova, Ukraine exempt from the hour minimum but must still prove experience.
- Demonstrated leadership experience from an employer or civil society engagement.
- Admitted (or conditionally admitted) to an eligible master's programme at admissions announcement; reserve-list placements don't count.
Documents: NONE verifiable on official sources — keep the honest empty state ("document list published only during the application call").

## 4. ETH Zurich ESOP
Source: https://ethz.ch/students/en/studies/financial/scholarships/excellencescholarship.html
Gates:
- Very good Bachelor's result — top 10% (grade A) by self-assessment.
- Not already enrolled in an ETH Zurich Master's, and not already holding a Master's degree.
- Apply within the first window: 1–30 November (next: 1–30 Nov 2026 for HS27 start).
- Apply for an ETH Zurich Master's programme via eApply.
Documents:
- Pre-proposal for the Master's thesis (self-developed, proper scientific citation — plagiarism means exclusion).
- Two reference letters from professors (uploaded via the system, if the programme doesn't ask for its own).
- The standard Master admission dossier (required "in addition" to the pre-proposal).
- CV.

## 6. Rhodes Scholarship — Hong Kong SAR (2027 cycle, closed 30 Sep 2026)
Source: https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/files/hongkonginformationforcandidates/
Gates:
- Hong Kong permanent resident, OR a Chinese citizen studying at / holding an Honours degree from a Hong Kong university, OR 7+ years living in Hong Kong with a strong personal connection.
- Aged 18–23 on 1 Oct 2026; or under 27 with the first undergraduate degree completed on/after 1 Oct 2025.
- Undergraduate degree completed (or completing by July 2027).
- Academic standing meeting the chosen Oxford course's entry requirements (First Class Honours / GPA ≥ 3.70 improves chances).
- Meet Oxford's higher-level English requirements, or qualify for a test waiver.
Documents:
- Birth certificate (age proof).
- Hong Kong Identity Card / passport copy (translation if needed).
- Official university transcript.
- Oxford-recognised English test results (e.g. IELTS), or a waiver request with evidence.
- Head-and-shoulders colour photograph (jpg).
Note: CV is completed inside the application form (no separate upload); Academic + Personal statements and 4–5 referees also required.

## 9. Rhodes Scholarship — United States (2027 cycle, closed 7 Oct 2026)
Source: https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/files/usainformationforcandidates/
Gates:
- US citizen, lawful permanent resident (status maintained), or active DACA holder.
- Apply as a representative of one state/territory (two years of college + bachelor's there, or legal residence there on 15 April of the application year); one district only.
- Aged 18–23 on 1 Oct 2026; or under 27 with the first undergraduate degree completed on/after 1 Oct 2025.
- Undergraduate degree completed by July 2027; GPA ≥ 3.70/4.0 with no rounding (below 3.70 needs a written exception request from the university president).
- English evidence NOT required in the Rhodes application itself (still required at the Oxford university-application stage).
Documents:
- Passport, birth certificate, or government ID (age + nationality); LPRs add unexpired Form I-551; DACA adds I-797 + Employment Authorization Card.
- Official university transcript.
- Endorsement from your college/university (separate from references).
- Full CV (max 2 letter-size pages, min 10pt, no photo; ORCID optional).
- Head-and-shoulders colour photograph (jpg).
Note: Academic Statement (max 450 words), Personal Statement (max 1000 words, unedited by others), 5–8 referees also required.

## 10. GKS-U 2027 — Pakistan (embassy track, closed 30 Sep 2026 18:00 KST; quota: General 2 + R-GKS 1)
Source: NIIED 2027 GKS-U Guidelines — https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/cmm/fms/FileDown.do?atchFileId=FILE_000000000673717&fileSn=1
Gates:
- Citizenship of an NIIED-invited country; parents/guardians must not hold Korean citizenship (dual Korean citizenship disqualifies; renunciation needs Korean-government proof).
- Under 25 — born on or after March 1, 2002.
- Graduated (or graduating by Dec 31, 2026) from high school or an associate programme; bachelor's holders cannot apply.
- Grades: 80+/100, or top 20% of class, or CGPA ≥ 2.64/4.0 (2.80/4.3, 2.91/4.5, 3.23/5.0).
- Good mental and physical health for the full programme in Korea.
- One application only — embassy track, one programme; duplicates are disregarded.
Documents:
- Application Form (Form 1) — completed online, printed and signed.
- Personal Statement (Form 2).
- Study Plan (Form 3).
- One Recommendation Letter (Form 4).
- Proof of citizenship (applicant + parents) and family relationship proof.
Note: Forms 5–7 (Agreement, Medical Assessment, Privacy Consent), graduation certificate + transcript (apostilled/consular-confirmed) also required; TOPIK/English scores and up to 5 award certificates optional.

## 14. Australia Awards — Nauru
Sources: https://nauru.highcommission.gov.au/files/nuru/Australia-Awards-2022-Brochure.pdf and https://www.dfat.gov.au/sites/default/files/aus-awards-scholarships-policy-handbook.pdf
Gates:
- Citizen of Nauru.
- Meet the Policy Handbook's general requirements: 18+ on 1 Feb of the commencement year; not an Australian citizen/PR/visa applicant; not partnered with an Australian/NZ citizen or PR; not serving military; no criminal convictions.
- Bachelor's: QCE / Australian Year 12 / Fiji Year 13 with aggregate ≥ 200/400 (English + best three subjects); must not hold an equivalent-or-higher qualification.
- Master's: first degree in a related field + 2 years' relevant work experience; must not hold a Master's already.
- At least 12 months' residence in Nauru before accepting.
- Not under any bond at applications close; previous recipients must have completed return periods.
Documents:
- Proof of citizenship (certified birth certificate or passport; photo ID if no passport).
- Certified academic transcripts (English translation if not in English).
- CV / resume.
- Employment referee report (signed, dated, officially stamped, on the Australia Awards template).
- English test result (IELTS 6.5 no band below 6.0, or TOEFL/PTE equivalent); shortlisted applicants without one must sit the test.
Note: Nauru-specific rules verified from the Intake 2022 brochure (latest verifiable official Nauru source; current DFAT Nauru intake page unreachable).
